Box2theBeat

Motiv8 Gym, Chippenham

Book Your Spot

Box2theBeat is a high energy, feel-good fitness class that combines boxing-inspired moves with great music to create a workout that feels more like a party than a gym session. No boxing experience needed whatsoever. You'll punch, move and groove your way through the session with a smile on your face the whole time. It's fun, it's sweaty and it's one of the most joyful ways to move your body.

Snap Fitness Melksham

Dance Fit and Step Fit

Snap Fitness, Melksham

Dance Fit and Step Fit are energetic, feel-good classes held at Snap Fitness Melksham. Dance Fit gets you moving to great music with easy-to-follow choreography that anyone can enjoy, while Step Fit is a classic step aerobics class with a modern twist. Both classes are free for Snap Fitness members and available on a pay as you go basis for non-members. Contact the gym directly to book your PAYG spot or book through the Snap app if you're a member.

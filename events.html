What is it

Less gym. More party.

RW Move & Groove is a 90-minute fitness experience that blends three of my signature class formats - DanceFit, StepFit and Box2theBeat - set to a live DJ set, with dance floor lighting, brand partners on site and goody bags to take home. Think rave brunch energy. A fitness experience that doesn't fit the gym, doesn't fit the night club, and lives somewhere joyfully in between. Built around community, music, movement and a celebration of women showing up for themselves on their own terms.

The Format

Three formats. One experience.

01

DanceFit

Easy-to-follow choreography set to music that makes you want to move. No experience needed - just bring your energy and let the music do the rest.

02

StepFit

Classic step aerobics with a modern twist. High energy, great rhythm, and the kind of cardio that doesn't feel like cardio when you're in the room.

03

Box2theBeat

Boxing-inspired moves set to banging music. More party than punch bag - you'll be moving, sweating and smiling the whole way through.

The Running Order

How the day flows

Doors

Welcome & Warm Up

Arrive, pick up your goody bag, explore the brand partners on site and ease in with a warm-up set as the DJ gets going.

Block 1

DanceFit

We kick things off on the dance floor. Easy moves, great music, instant energy.

Transition

Free Dance Break

Between blocks the class becomes a dance floor. No choreography, no instruction - just you and the music.

Block 2

Box2theBeat

We bring the energy up another level. Punching to the beat, great music, the room is fully alive.

Transition

Free Dance Break

Another moment to just move however you want before the final block.

Block 3

StepFit

We finish strong. Step to the beat, full energy, best track of the set. The room will be electric.

Hang Time

Cool Down, Photos & Connection

We cool down together and then the floor is yours - photos, sampling brand partners, catching up with the community. No rush to leave.
